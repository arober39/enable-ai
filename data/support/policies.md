# Serenia & Co. Support Policies (v2026.04)

This document is the source of truth for customer-facing policies the Support team enforces. The runtime support orchestrator cites this file when drafting policy-grounded responses.

## Refund authority tiers

| Tier                | Authority limit | Approver                               |
|---------------------|-----------------|----------------------------------------|
| Tier-1 specialist   | Up to $500      | Self (Essentials), with audit log      |
| Tier-1 specialist   | Up to $500      | Self (Signature)                       |
| Tier-1 specialist   | Up to $1,000    | Self (Enterprise)                      |
| Tier-2 team lead    | Up to $5,000    | Self, notifies VP                      |
| VP / Finance        | Above $5,000    | Finance approves; Legal notified above $25k |

A refund issued at any tier must include an internal note citing the specific policy clause or contract section that justifies it. Refunds for vendor underdelivery are reconciled against the vendor's contract separately by vendor relations.

## Cancellation refund schedule

The customer's contract governs in any conflict, but the standard schedule is:

| Days before event | Refund of paid amounts (excludes non-refundable deposit) |
|-------------------|----------------------------------------------------------|
| 90+               | 100%                                                     |
| 60-89             | 75%                                                      |
| 30-59             | 50%                                                      |
| 14-29             | 25%                                                      |
| 0-13              | 0%                                                       |

The non-refundable deposit covers venue hold, event lead time, and the contracts Serenia locks with vendors on the customer's behalf in the first 90 days. It is **never refundable** except in the case of Serenia breach.

Committed vendor deposits are recoverable only to the extent the vendor itself returns them. Vendor relations attempts recovery in all cancellations.

## Vendor substitution

When a confirmed vendor cancels or fails to deliver, Serenia commits to:

1. Acknowledge within **2 business hours** during business hours, or by 9am next day if outside.
2. Provide a shortlist of replacement vendors within **24 hours**.
3. Absorb any cost delta between the original and replacement vendor when the original vendor's failure is unilateral (not driven by customer change).
4. Cost deltas driven by customer-initiated changes (scope, date, headcount) are billed to the customer at the new vendor's rate.

## Scope changes

Customer-initiated scope changes are accommodated based on lead time:

- **More than 30 days out:** generally accommodable. Event lead scopes within 3 business days.
- **14-30 days out:** accommodable case-by-case. Catering, AV, and venue capacity often constrain. Event lead provides a feasibility read within 1 business day.
- **7-13 days out:** limited. Headcount additions over 5 require caterer exception (not always granted). Menu changes generally not possible.
- **Under 7 days:** emergencies only (medical, weather, vendor cancellation). Cosmetic changes deferred.

## Response time SLAs

| Plan tier   | First response (business hours) | Resolution target for tier-1 |
|-------------|---------------------------------|------------------------------|
| Essentials  | 4 hours                         | 2 business days              |
| Signature   | 2 hours                         | 1 business day               |
| Enterprise  | 1 hour                          | Same business day            |

Out-of-hours tickets are acknowledged the next business day at 9am local time. Tickets flagged URGENT by the customer are escalated to on-call team lead regardless of SLA.

## Escalation policy

A ticket is escalated to **Tier-2 (team lead)** when:

- The customer requests escalation by name.
- A refund or credit above the Tier-1 authority is required.
- The ticket touches a vendor cancellation, breach, or contract issue.
- The customer has escalated tone (multiple tickets, mentions of churn, mentions of legal/chargeback).
- A scope change inside the 14-day window is requested.

A ticket is escalated to **Legal** when:

- The customer mentions legal action, chargeback, or lawyer involvement.
- A vendor contract is alleged to be breached.
- A confidentiality, PII, or compliance incident is reported.

A ticket is escalated to **Finance** when:

- Refund authority above Tier-2.
- Disputed charges or billing reconciliation involving more than one invoice.
- Procurement or AP requests that affect payment processing.

## Compliance and security

For Enterprise customers, the trust team provides:

- SOC 2 Type II attestation (annually refreshed; current report as of February 2026).
- COI (Certificate of Insurance) on request.
- Background check policy for any Serenia on-site staff (Sterling 7-year criminal + ID verify).
- Standard Master Services Agreement template; redlines turned within 5 business days.

Customer PII (names, emails, dietary restrictions) is handled per Serenia's privacy policy. Customer data is never used to train external AI models.
